# Bio-Aware Blended Spaces (BABS)
## Potential Research Project Vectors

This document consolidates **potential semester-scale research project vectors** for the Bio-Aware Blended Spaces (BABS) meta-project. Each item represents a **coherent, ownable research responsibility** suitable for one student (or a small team) and designed to integrate into the larger BABS system.

Projects are organized by **system layer**, not by discipline or tool, reinforcing BABS as a *self-aware, integrated environment* rather than a collection of isolated experiments.

---

## A. Sensor Systems — Human and Environmental Awareness

### Human / Physiological Sensors
1. EEG Signal Acquisition via Muse 2 SDK into Max/MSP + Jitter
2. Heart Rate and HRV Capture Using Wearable or Chest-Strap Sensors
3. Galvanic Skin Response (GSR) as Arousal and Engagement Signal
4. Breath and Pulse Detection Using Contact Microphones and Piezo Sensors

### Environmental & Spatial Sensors (Room Self-Awareness)
5. Distributed Area Microphone System for Room Acoustic Awareness
6. Ambient Sound Energy and Spectral Density Mapping of Shared Space
7. Temperature and Thermal Gradient Sensing Across the Room
8. Humidity and Air Quality (CO₂ / VOC) Monitoring as Environmental State
9. Barometric Pressure and Atmospheric Drift as Contextual Signal
10. Light Level and Color Temperature Sensing for Environmental Context

### Vision-Based Sensors
11. Camera-Based Respiration and Micro-Movement Detection
12. Depth-Camera Occupancy and Posture Mapping
13. Collective Motion Density Estimation via Computer Vision
